Glass palette with recent commands. Demo only: the page links point back to this note, the two actions emit iv:command and nothing else, and the same destinations are part of the normal navigation of the page. What you activate is kept in localStorage under iv-command-recent and comes back as a “Recent” group the next time the palette opens with an empty search. / is switched off here; Ctrl + K and ⌘ + K still open it.
