Sample copy for the fixture. The bar pinned to the top of the window is .iv-scroll-progress: it is painted full width once and scaled horizontally, so a single composited property changes as the page moves. On an engine with animation-timeline: scroll(root) the stylesheet drives it alone; elsewhere ScrollMotion writes --iv-scroll on this section and the bar inherits it.

The bar is decoration and says so: aria-hidden="true". The reading position is already available to assistive technology through the scrollbar and the document structure, and a second announcement would only be noise.

What to watch

Scroll to the end of this fixture: the bar reaches the full width of the window exactly when the document does. Halfway down it covers half the window.
