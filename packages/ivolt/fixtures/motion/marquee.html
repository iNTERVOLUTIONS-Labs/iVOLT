A marquee is pure CSS: the author writes the items twice and hides the copy from assistive technology, so the track is exactly two loops wide and a translation of −50 % is seamless. It pauses under the pointer and while anything inside it holds focus, and under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce it stops and becomes a scrollable row — which is why the author serves tabindex="0", a role and a label on the window, exactly as the horizontal timeline does.
