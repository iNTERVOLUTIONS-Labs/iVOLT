Room to scroll

The text below exists so the condensed state can be seen: it has no meaning of its own. Scroll down and the bar loses height, takes a glass background, an ambient shadow and the conductive rule along its bottom edge.

The brand and the links keep their size; only the bar's height and its surface change, so nothing jumps under the pointer. The underline of a link grows from the centre on hover and on keyboard focus, and the current page keeps it drawn.

On a narrow screen the panel folds. Opening it turns the burger into a cross, the panel slides in from above and its height is capped to the viewport minus the bar, so a long menu scrolls inside itself instead of pushing the page.

Escape closes the panel and returns the focus to the burger, a click outside closes it too, and crossing the breakpoint closes it and hands the layout back to the bar.

Nothing here is fetched, stored or sent. The fixture is one header and this filler.
