Sample copy for the fixture. .iv-text-glow is two layers of text-shadow around the glyphs: a tight one that thickens the strokes and a wide one that spills onto the surface behind. The colour is the halo token of the theme, so it changes with light and dark.

Voltage in the type

And the same line, breathing

The pulse runs for 2.4 seconds and repeats; under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce it stops at the glow itself. In forced colours the shadow is dropped and the heading is plain system text.

Over a gradient surface

Both --iv-text-glow-color and --iv-text-glow-size are public locals; here the halo is the secondary one and a little wider.

A halo is a finish, not a contrast device: the glyph itself keeps the contrast of the theme, and the glow only adds presence around it.
