Sample controls: none of the buttons on this page does anything. Point at one and its label appears after a moment; move the keyboard focus to it and the label appears at once. Escape dismisses it.

The label is complementary: every control above is also readable without it, because without JavaScript there is no tooltip at all. The text lives in data-iv-tooltip and reaches assistive technology through aria-describedby while it is on screen.
